# How to Use the AI Executive Meeting Brief

## Purpose

This workflow helps transform meeting information into a concise executive briefing.

## Step 1 — Collect Meeting Information

Gather the available information about the meeting, such as:

- Meeting objective
- Attendees
- Previous meeting context
- Current updates
- Open questions
- Decisions required
- Follow-up items

## Step 2 — Provide the Information to the AI

Copy the meeting information and provide it to an AI assistant together with the instructions contained in:

`meeting_brief_prompt.md`

## Step 3 — Generate the Executive Brief

Ask the AI to generate an executive meeting brief following the instructions.

## Step 4 — Review the Output

The executive assistant should review the generated brief for:

- Accuracy
- Missing information
- Important decisions
- Action items
- Numbers and factual details
- Context that may require clarification

AI output should be reviewed by a human before being used for decision-making.

## Step 5 — Use the Brief

The final brief can be used to help the executive:

- Prepare for the meeting
- Identify important questions
- Understand decisions that may be required
- Track follow-up actions

## Example

A complete example is available in the `examples` folder.

The example demonstrates the transformation from meeting information into a structured executive briefing.
