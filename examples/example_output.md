# Example Executive Meeting Brief

## Meeting Objective

Review the quarterly budget and determine whether the requested AED 150,000 additional operational budget should be approved.

## Key Context

Operational costs increased by 8% compared with the previous quarter, while revenue remained stable.

The Operations team has requested an additional AED 150,000 for the next quarter to support planned growth. Finance has not yet approved the request.

## Important Information

- Operational costs are increasing faster than revenue.
- The additional budget request is AED 150,000.
- The Operations team considers the additional funding necessary for planned growth.
- Finance has not yet approved the request.
- Updated cost projections will be provided after the meeting.

## Decisions Required

1. Approve or reject the additional AED 150,000 operational budget.
2. Determine whether cost-reduction measures are necessary.

## Questions to Consider

- What specifically caused the 8% increase in operational costs?
- Which expenses could be reduced or postponed?
- What financial return or business impact is expected from the additional AED 150,000?
- What would be the financial impact of rejecting the request?
- Are there alternative ways to support the planned growth?

## Action Items

- Finance Director to provide updated cost projections after the meeting.
- Leadership team to determine whether the additional budget should be approved.
- Finance and Operations to evaluate potential cost-reduction opportunities if required.

## Missing Information

- Detailed breakdown of the 8% increase in operational costs.
- Financial projections associated with the planned growth.
- Detailed justification for the AED 150,000 request.
- Potential cost-saving alternatives.
